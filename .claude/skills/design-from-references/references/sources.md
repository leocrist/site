# Reference sources

Baseline inspected: 2026-11-05. Treat categories and access as a dated discovery map, not guaranteed future functionality. Verify current access at use. The galleries contain third-party designs; use original principles, not unlicensed assets. Never promise editable downloads from a visual gallery.

## 1. Land-book — https://land-book.com
- Coverage observed: Websites, Sections, Mobile, Motion, OG Images, Headlines. Filters: industry, style, type, typography, colour, platform.
- Section examples: hero, footer, CTA, FAQ, resources, testimonial, value proposition, features, brands.
- Explore: match industry/content density first, then style and typography. Inspect a full page plus relevant sections. Use Headlines for message hierarchy when rewriting is permitted.
- Extract: section sequence, grid, spacing rhythm, type relationships, image placement, headline/supporting-copy relationship.
- Apply: coherent overall website direction and reusable section patterns. Do not infer app workflow completeness from website screenshots.
- Public detail seed: https://land-book.com/websites/102061-relume-ai-website-builder-for-marketing-websites (sections and headlines were exposed). Verify relevance instead of choosing it automatically.
- Entry point: https://land-book.com/sections

## 2. Supahero — https://supahero.io
- Coverage observed: hero gallery; individual entries show hero image, description and original-site link. The site announces it is part of Screensdesign.
- Explore: inspect headline, supporting message, CTA, image/product depiction and balance of the first screen. Compare a few compositions compatible with the user's actual copy length.
- Extract: eye path, first impression, value proposition, CTA hierarchy, visual emphasis.
- Apply: a focused first screen; do not treat it as a full landing-page strategy.
- Public detail seed: https://supahero.io/hero/arc
- Limitation: do not assume old filters or current access to the destination service.

## 3. Pricing Pages — https://pricingpages.design
- Coverage observed: tier cards, feature/checkmark tables, comparison tables, monthly/annual switch, custom/enterprise pricing, contact sales, FAQs, testimonials and usage-based calculator patterns.
- Explore: match the billing model first; compare simple versus information-heavy pages; inspect desktop/mobile images when available.
- Extract: plan differentiation, recommended-tier emphasis, units and billing clarity, comparison density, objections handled near decision points.
- Apply: clear purchase/selection decisions using supplied prices and terms only. A calculator screenshot does not provide calculation logic.
- Public detail seed: https://pricingpages.design/sites/huddlekit (desktop/mobile images and style tags). A stray template heading was observed; judge the actual entry rather than unrelated headings.

## 4. Social Proof Examples — https://socialproofexamples.com
- Coverage observed: explained examples including walls of results/impact, third-party badges, quantified adoption, user stories, character-portrait videos and social-proof ads.
- Explore: select evidence addressing a real objection: credibility, outcomes, relevance, or reliability.
- Extract: evidence type, attribution, context, placement relative to a claim, balance of qualitative and quantitative proof.
- Apply: verified testimonials, customer evidence, appropriately contextualised results. For academic work prefer real findings, sources and affiliations; never invent commercial endorsements.
- Limitation: descriptive explanations are not experimental evidence that a pattern improves conversion.

## 5. Really Good Emails — https://reallygoodemails.com
- Coverage observed: welcome, onboarding, newsletters, launch, promotion, event, transactional, confirmation, reminder, re-engagement and more. Categories: https://reallygoodemails.com/categories
- Detail views observed: Desktop, Mobile, Code, Text, Info; availability can vary.
- Explore: match the reader's lifecycle stage and single intended action; compare full content sequence and mobile version.
- Extract: subject/preheader relationship where shown, opening block, image/text balance, modules, CTA timing, footer.
- Apply: a purpose-specific email or sequence. Do not confuse inspiration-gallery capabilities with RGE Studio export/authoring features or guarantee HTML compatibility from a preview.
- Public detail seed: https://reallygoodemails.com/emails/norda-welcome-trail-running

## 6. Deck.gallery — https://www.deck.gallery
- Coverage observed: full decks and individual slides; pitch, guideline, report, talks, portfolio, marketing, sales, strategy, proposal; industry filters.
- Explore: use full decks for story sequence, individual slides for layout problems. Match audience and information density, rather than using pitch-deck conventions for every academic talk.
- Extract: opening, section dividers, narrative pacing, recurring layouts, data density, hierarchy and closing.
- Apply: a coherent editable slide system rebuilt in the requested format.
- Limitations: site explicitly describes slides as images, not editable files. Some content/features require membership. Do not claim a whole deck was inspected if only thumbnails were accessible.

## 7. Posts.design — https://posts.design
- Coverage observed: Posts, Headers, OGs; search by post/brand; categories including launches, hiring, event, update, teaser, partnership, branding, content, milestone, ad, award, chart, feature, poster and more.
- Explore: match communication purpose, compare several posts from one brand when building a series, inspect imagery at feed size.
- Extract: message density, logo placement, type hierarchy, repeated brand devices and variation across a campaign.
- Apply: social announcements, event graphics, headers and branded content systems. Do not assume every category result is accurately tagged; inspect the actual post.
- Entry point: https://posts.design/categories/event — describes invitations, schedules and recaps for conferences/workshops/live sessions.

## 8. Adfolio — https://www.adfolio.design
- Coverage observed: curated B2B SaaS advertising, with Meta/LinkedIn and landing-page inspiration; the site describes hook/visual/CTA analysis.
- Explore: inspect actual creative or available breakdown, not a company-logo thumbnail. Match awareness stage, offer and channel.
- Extract: hook, benefit, visual metaphor/product demonstration, supporting evidence, CTA and message continuity to landing page.
- Apply: B2B SaaS concepts; transfer structural principles cautiously to other audiences and industries.
- Limitations: do not infer CTR, ROAS, CPA, or causal effectiveness from curation or the site's promotional descriptions.

## 9. Navbar Gallery — https://www.navbar.gallery
- Coverage observed: static/sticky, dropdown/flyout, mega menu, sidebar, search, announcement bar, fullscreen, breadcrumbs; additional tags such as tabs/progress bars appear in listings.
- Explore: match information architecture and number of destinations. Inspect desktop/mobile images; follow original site for necessary interaction behaviour.
- Extract: grouping, label clarity, logo/action balance, current-location cues and mobile simplification.
- Apply: navigation suited to actual user tasks. Verify focus, keyboard control, expanded state, closing behaviour and scrolling in implementation.
- Public detail seed: https://www.navbar.gallery/navbar/supaste — desktop/mobile images and a static-navbar classification.

## 10. OGimage.gallery — https://www.ogimage.gallery
- Coverage observed: link-preview images with categories including Animated, Dynamic, App, Arts & Culture, Creative, Finance, Food, Health, Portfolios, Resources, SaaS and Stores.
- Explore: inspect actual image, not merely destination website; match content type and brand prominence.
- Extract: title length, focal image, contrast, logo location and legibility at small scale.
- Apply: a compact preview composition for shared links, including reusable templates where requested.
- Limitations: verify target-platform dimensions, crop and metadata requirements separately; animation support varies and cannot be assumed. An inspiration image alone does not implement Open Graph metadata.
