Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you need?
Call 0939 980 090

What documents should I bring to a service point?

Your chip-based citizen ID card covers most procedures: opening an account, activating VNeID level 2, buying vehicle insurance. For a claim, also bring your vehicle registration and insurance certificate.

Is buying insurance through Minh Group different from buying directly from PVI or PTI?

Policies are issued by PVI and PTI themselves, with the same benefits. The difference is a local advisor in your commune who handles renewals, reminders and claims, so you don’t have to call a hotline yourself.

Do you charge fees upfront for the Europe work programme?

Consultation and the initial document check are free. Fees apply only after the contract is signed, within the legal caps and itemised in the contract. Overseas employment service licence: [Licence no.].

Which licence classes does the driving school teach?

We currently train classes A1, A, B (manual and automatic) and C1 under the 2024 Road Traffic Order and Safety Law, with evening and weekend classes for working people.

What do I need to become an agent or point of sale?

A stable business premises and a person in charge with a smartphone. Minh Group provides training, an account and point-of-sale materials.

How soon will someone call me back?

During business hours (7:30 – 17:30, Mon – Sat), a local advisor calls back within 30 minutes. Outside hours, we call at the start of the next working session.
