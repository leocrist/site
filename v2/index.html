Good to know

Questions CTOs ask us first

How do you protect our IP and data?

Our Can Tho delivery centre runs an ISMS certified to ISO/IEC 27001 and independently audited by CPG (certificate ISMS/20/R84/1419). Every engagement runs in isolated environments, under NDA, with contracts governed by Australian law.

How much do our teams actually overlap?

Can Tho is three to four hours behind Melbourne, depending on Australian daylight saving. That leaves a shared block of live hours every working day for stand-ups, reviews and decisions.

Who is accountable if something goes wrong?

Our Melbourne leadership. Solution architecture, engagement management and client accountability sit in Australia; delivery pods report into an Australian tech lead.

Do we have to replace our existing team or tools?

No. Pods join your sprint rituals and work in your GitHub, GitLab or AWS CodePipeline setup. The AI tooling plugs into the pipeline you already run.

How quickly can a pod start?

Typically within weeks, after a short discovery phase to agree scope, team shape and governance. We confirm the date in the proposal.
