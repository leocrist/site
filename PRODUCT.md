# PTN Global: product context

register: brand

## Product purpose
Marketing website for PTN Global, a technology engineering partner. It is founded and led from Melbourne, with its delivery centre in Can Tho, Vietnam, and is ISO/IEC 27001 certified (CPG, ISMS/20/R84/1419). The site has to win discovery calls from enterprise buyers.

## Users
- **Primary:** CTOs, heads of engineering and transformation leads at Australian mid-market and enterprise organisations, typically comparing several offshore partners. They are sceptical of vendor claims and care about IP, security, governance and time-zone overlap.
- **Secondary:** engineers and designers in Can Tho evaluating PTN as an employer (Careers).

## Scene
A CTO in a bright Melbourne office mid-morning, comparing offshore partners in browser tabs, sceptical. That rules out a dark theme: the site is a light canvas with committed PTN deep-blue chapters.

## Brand
- Logo: PTN Global (blue #008FC4, green #A7BC4D, black wordmark).
- Tone: plain, specific and accountable. Australian spelling (modernise, organisation).
- Terminology: "delivery pod", "Melbourne-led, delivered from Can Tho", "AI-Augmented Engineering Model".

## Anti-references
- Generic AI SaaS: purple gradients, glass cards, neon-on-black, hero-metric tiles.
- Fabricated proof: invented logos, numbers or testimonials. Conflicting figures must be confirmed before they are shown.
- Copying clickup.com. Use its principles (light canvas, tight display type, product-style hero, tabbed dark chapter), not its assets or palette.

## Strategic principles
1. Show the evidence: certificate links, named testimonials, and numbers with their source.
2. Every CTA leads to a conversation, with the topic pre-filled.
3. Time-zone overlap and governance are the differentiators, so show them concretely.
