Our story

Founded in Melbourne, built in Vietnam.

PTN Global was founded by Tam Phan who is based in Melbourne, Australia. Tam has worked in the IT industry for almost 30 years and was the Managing Director of TMA Solutions Australia between 2011 and 2019. Prior to TMA Solutions, Tam was the Business Lead of the Telstra Self Service Channel Transformation Program where he then went on to become the Co-Founder and CTO of eXceed Global.

Tam has always been passionate about providing solutions in the IT industry. His experience has helped him identify opportunities to solve the shortage of qualified IT personnel and stringent time to market by crafting a hybrid model that seeks better results and outputs with lesser business risk and costs for his clients.

PTN Global’s Main Office is based in Melbourne, Australia. The Development Center is located in Vietnam. With our offshore team located in Vietnam, we provide the very best combination of offshore expertise and face-to-face interactions to make sure you always know who it is that you are dealing with.

Methodologies

Methodologies that drive technical success

Tailored development approaches for each project: Agile, Scrum, Kanban, DevOps, Lean, SAFe and XP.

Agile: Flexibility for Rapid Innovation

At PTN, we use Agile to deliver in short cycles, adapt quickly to changing requirements, and keep products aligned with evolving business goals.

Scrum: Structured, High-Quality Delivery

Scrum ensures clear deliverables and incremental value at every sprint, helping projects stay on schedule while maintaining quality.

Kanban: Transparent, Efficient Workflows

Kanban provides visibility into progress, highlights bottlenecks, and optimises task flow for greater efficiency and transparency.

DevOps: Continuous Integration & Deployment

Our DevOps practices automate testing, monitoring, and deployment, ensuring reliable releases and faster time-to-market.

Lean: Efficiency and Maximum Value

Lean helps us eliminate waste and focus resources on what matters most-delivering high-value software within budget.

SAFe: Scaling Agile Across Teams

SAFe enables us to align multiple teams and stakeholders, ensuring consistency and collaboration in large-scale projects.

XP: Technical Excellence & Speed

With practices like pair programming, TDD, and continuous integration, XP keeps our code clean, scalable, and ready for rapid iteration.
