Collaboration

How our roles collaborate to turn your vision into reality

More than a process: your path to seamless team expansion.

  1. 1

    Discovery & Requirement Gathering

    BAs meet with the client to understand business goals, user needs, and product vision. Create requirement documents, use cases, and workflows.

  2. 2

    UX/UI Design

    Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes. Collaborate with BA to ensure alignment with user flows and platform logic.

  3. 3

    Architecture & Planning

    DevOps design the system architecture, database schema, infrastructure needs. Set up CI/CD pipelines, hosting plans.

  4. 4

    Frontend & Backend Development

    Build out the core functionality (BE) and interactive user interfaces (FE), based on designs and requirements.

  5. 5

    QA & Testing

    Test for bugs, performance issues, and UX problems. Ensure all features meet the original requirements.

  6. 6

    Deployment & Monitoring

    Deploy the product to staging and production environments. Monitor uptime, performance, and scalability.

Quality

Quality commitment

Tailored software for diverse industries, delivered under a quality management approach and a certified information security management system.

Quality Management System

  • Achieve outcomes aligned with international standards.
  • Deliver consistent, reliable quality.
  • Reduce risks by identifying issues early and preventing recurring errors.
  • Follow structured procedures to optimize resources and time.

Information security management

  • Protect people, processes, and technology through three core principles:
  • Confidentiality: Information is accessible only to authorized individuals.
  • Integrity: Information can be modified only by authorized individuals.
  • Availability: Information remains accessible to authorized individuals whenever needed.
